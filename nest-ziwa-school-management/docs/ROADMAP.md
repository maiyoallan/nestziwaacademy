# Implementation roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (included)
- Authentication, roles, audit trail and base database schema
- Dashboard and reusable module records workspace
- Local PostgreSQL setup and seed administrator

## Phase 2 — Core school operations
- Admissions workflow and student/guardian linking
- Class, stream, academic year and enrolment workflows
- Student/staff attendance and timetables
- Teacher class assignments and permissions

## Phase 3 — Finance
- Fee structures by class/term
- Invoices, receipts, allocations, arrears and credit notes
- Cashbook, expenses, approvals and reconciliation
- M-Pesa integration with callback verification and idempotency

## Phase 4 — Academics
- Subject configuration, assessments, marks entry
- Grading scales, report cards and performance analysis
- Parent/student restricted portals

## Phase 5 — Operations
- HR, leave and payroll
- Stores, procurement and asset register
- Transport routes, vehicles and driver assignments
- SMS/email and document generation

## Phase 6 — Production readiness
- Row-level authorization and parent/student account linking
- LAN deployment and designed offline synchronization
- Backups/restore drills, monitoring, security review and acceptance testing
